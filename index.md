### Who's who

- **Judge** — U.S. District Judge Ana Reyes.
- **Plaintiffs:** Abbe Lowell (**AL**), who examines witnesses; Mark Samburg; Norm Eisen.
- **DOJ (government):** Stan Woodward (**SW**) leads. Parloff calls him "Associate AG" in [P2][] and "Assoc GC" in [P3][]. Also Robert Stander (**RS**), Andrew Block, Marissa Pilopato. "DOJ" means a government lawyer the reporters didn't identify.
- **Witnesses:**
  - Greg Wischer (**GW**), Department of the Interior (DOI) Chief of Staff. He also testifies he has been deputy chief of staff since May 2025.
  - Jessica Bowron (**JB**), acting director of the National Park Service (NPS).
  - Dr. Kevin Griess (**KG**), NPS.
- **Named in testimony:** Frank Lands, NPS deputy director (released without testifying today); Tom Fazio and his son Gavin Fazio, golf course architects; Natalie Harp, White House staff; Interior Secretary Doug Burgum; Mike Camisso, NPS (spelling uncertain); Tammy Stieham/Stimmon, NPS regional office (spelling uncertain).
- **Terms:**
  - **CLR** — the 2019 Cultural Landscape Report for DC's three public courses (East Potomac, Rock Creek, Langston).
  - **TMP** — the Tree Management Plan. Under it, roughly 400–500 trees were cut on 10/10.
  - **WNP** — the "Washington National" plan, Trump's championship-course redesign.
  - **NLT** — National Links Trust, the former operator of the courses.
  - **APA** — the Administrative Procedure Act.
  - **NEPA** — the National Environmental Policy Act.
  - **NHPA** — the National Historic Preservation Act.
  - **PI** — preliminary injunction.

## How this was compiled

- **Source collection:** every post was pulled from Bluesky's public API on Oct 19, 2026. Bluesky's thread view stopped early, after 11 posts, so both reporters' full post histories for the day were fetched instead.
  - Parloff: kept only posts in his hearing thread (188).
  - Roberts: kept everything she posted between 9:41 a.m. and 3:41 p.m. ET (220), covering her two hearing threads plus her opening pointer to Parloff.
- **Timestamps:** converted from UTC to Eastern Daylight Time (UTC−4). Entries are grouped by the hour the posts were published.
- **Merging:** where both reporters covered the same exchange, their posts are combined into one entry citing both. Each entry gives the fullest wording either reporter captured. Details only one reporter caught are added and attributed: many of the 👀 moments, plus quips like "A good walk ruined" and "heated agreement."
- **Conflicts:** where the accounts differ, both versions are shown with an *(ed.)* note: the "noon tomorrow" deadline, the Reflecting Pool vs. Grand Prix crew, and name spellings.
- **Cleanup:**
  - Typos fixed and shorthand expanded ("atty," "dec," "YH," "Idk").
  - Speaker labels standardized.
  - Quotes kept as the reporters posted them, with only spelling fixed.
  - Nothing was added that isn't in a post. Compiler notes are marked *(ed.)*.
- **Omitted:** off-topic replies to readers (e.g., [P95][] "Thanks, Lisa!"), housekeeping posts about broken threads ([R45][], [R85][]), and the posts that only number a thread's continuation.
- **Citations:** each `[P#]`/`[R#]` is the post's chronological position in that reporter's collected set. The links below resolve to the original posts.
- **Not a transcript:** this is a secondhand compilation of live paraphrase. For the official record, see the [court docket](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/72278895/dc-preservation-league-v-department-of-interior/?order_by=desc).
- **Tools:** the timeline was assembled with Claude Code (Anthropic) from the fetched posts. Every claim traces to a cited post.
