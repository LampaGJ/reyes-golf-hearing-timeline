# East Potomac Golf Course Hearing — Courtroom Timeline

**Case:** *DC Preservation League v. Department of the Interior* (D.D.C.), before Judge Ana Reyes
**Date:** Monday, October 19, 2026 (Columbus Day holiday)
**Source:** Live-blog thread by Roger Parloff (@rparloff.bsky.social) for Lawfare, posted from the courthouse media room — [thread root][#1]
**Coverage:** 9:39 a.m. – 4:08 p.m. ET (185 posts). The thread was still live when this was compiled; the hearing had broken until 4:20 p.m. for plaintiffs' argument.

## How to read this

- Times are **Eastern**, taken from each post's timestamp. A post lands a minute or two after the moment it describes.
- Every entry links to its source post as `[#n]` (the post's position in the thread).
- Dialogue is **Parloff's real-time paraphrase**, not a transcript. Parloff says more than once that he can't keep up ([#13], [#49], [#70], [#164]). Spelling and typos are cleaned up. Wording inside quotation marks is kept as he posted it.
- 👁 marks **nonverbal or procedural** moments: someone standing, a bench conference, a break, a note being passed, a witness being sent out.
- `[brackets]` are Parloff's asides, or mine where marked *(ed.)*.

### Who's who

- **Judge** — U.S. District Judge Ana Reyes.
- **Plaintiffs:** Abbe Lowell (**AL**), who examines witnesses; Mark Samburg; Norm Eisen.
- **DOJ (government):** Stan Woodward (**SW**) leads. Parloff calls him "Associate AG" in [#2] and "Assoc GC" in [#3]. Also Robert Stander (**RS**), Andrew Block, Marissa Pilopato. "DOJ" means a government lawyer Parloff couldn't identify.
- **Witnesses:**
  - Greg Wischer (**GW**), Department of the Interior (DOI) Chief of Staff. He also testifies he has been deputy chief of staff since May 2025.
  - Jessica Bowron (**JB**), acting director of the National Park Service (NPS).
  - Dr. Kevin Griess (**KG**), NPS.
- **Named in testimony:** Frank Lands, NPS deputy director (released without testifying today); Tom Fazio and his son Gavin Fazio, golf course architects; Natalie Harp, White House staff; Interior Secretary Doug Burgum.
- **Terms:**
  - **CLR** — the 2019 Cultural Landscape Report for DC's three public courses (East Potomac, Rock Creek, Langston).
  - **TMP** — the Tree Management Plan. Under it, roughly 400–500 trees were cut on 10/10.
  - **WNP** — the "Washington National" plan, Trump's championship-course redesign.
  - **NLT** — National Links Trust, the former operator of the courses.
  - **APA** — the Administrative Procedure Act.
  - **NEPA** — the National Environmental Policy Act.
  - **NHPA** — the National Historic Preservation Act.
